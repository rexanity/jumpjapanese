Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Published by: Rex Chung ("we", "us"), publisher of Jump Japanese

Contact: https://github.com/rexanity/jumpjapanese/issues

The short version

  • There is no account, no sign-up, no email, no password.
  • Your study progress stays on your phone.
  • We do not run ads, we do not track you across apps or sites, and we do not sell data. Optional anonymous usage counts and crash reports are OFF unless you switch them on in Settings (see "Optional anonymous usage and crash reports").
  • The microphone is used only when you tap to practise pronunciation or speaking. The recording is processed on your phone and is never uploaded.
  • The app connects to the internet only when you choose to download the speech voice pack or the optional AI tutor model (see "What leaves your device"). Nothing else is sent today.
  • To delete your data, reset progress in Settings or uninstall the app.

What the apps are

Jump Japanese N5 and Jump Japanese N4 are offline study apps for the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT). Lessons, questions and lesson audio are packaged inside the app. The speech-recognition model used for pronunciation practice (about 240 MB) is not in the download from the app store: the app downloads it the first time you use pronunciation practice (the "voice pack"). Until then, the rest of the app works offline.

Information stored on your device

The app saves this on your phone only (in the app's private storage):

  • Your chosen interface language, study level, target exam date, and (if you pick one) work sector and avatar.
  • Your diagnostic result, study plan, completed tasks, streak, review cards, drill statistics and mock-exam history.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. This data is not sent to us and we cannot see it.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone permission for pronunciation practice ("shadowing") and speaking practice. When you tap record:

  • Audio is captured to a temporary file in the app's private storage and turned into text by a speech model that runs on your phone.
  • The audio and text are not uploaded and are not shared with us or anyone else.
  • The app deletes the temporary file as soon as transcription finishes (or fails). If the app is force-closed mid-transcription, the file stays in the app's private cache until the operating system clears it or you uninstall the app.

You can refuse or later revoke microphone permission in your phone's settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

What leaves your device

  1. Voice-pack download (speech recognition). When you choose to download the voice pack, the app fetches the model files (about 240 MB) over HTTPS from Cloudflare R2, a file-hosting service operated by Cloudflare, Inc. (privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). The host can see your IP address and basic request details such as device type and the file requested. The app sends no study data, recordings or identifiers in these requests. The files are checked against a built-in checksum before use. After the download, speech recognition runs entirely on your phone.
  2. Optional AI tutor model download. If you open the conversation tutor and choose to download it, the app downloads a language-model file (about 1.1 GB) from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any download, the host can see your IP address and basic request details such as device type. The app sends no study data, recordings or identifiers in this request. After the download, the tutor runs entirely on your phone and your messages to it are not sent anywhere. Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to that download.
  3. App store traffic. Downloading, updating, and paying for apps goes through Apple or Google, under their policies. We receive only the aggregate reports they provide.
  4. Operating-system speech. Lesson audio is recorded into the app. For any sentence without a recording, the app uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. That is part of your operating system and not controlled by us.

We do not use advertising or attribution services, and no analytics or crash-reporting service is connected to the app today.

Optional anonymous usage and crash reports

Settings has a switch, "Share anonymous usage", which is off by default. The app contains the code for it, but at the time of writing no vendor is connected, so nothing is transmitted even if you switch it on.

If we connect a reporting service in a future version, then only for people who switch it on:

  • The app would send a fixed list of events with counts and categories only, for example: app opened, onboarding finished, diagnostic started/finished (with a score range), study plan created (with days to exam), lesson finished, drill session finished (cards and correct counts), mock exam finished (pass or not and percentage), voice pack downloaded, study-streak milestone, plus your study level and interface language, and crash reports.
  • It would never include your answers, typed text, recordings, name, email or contact details.
  • The service provider would see your IP address as part of receiving the data.
  • Switching it off stops sending immediately.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not receive any.

Retention and deletion

Your data lives only on your phone. You can delete it at any time:

  • Settings → Reset progress clears your progress, or
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all app data, including any downloaded voice pack and tutor model.

Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore it for you. Uninstalling or changing phones starts you from zero.

Children

The apps are designed for adults preparing for the JLPT and are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent and have a concern, contact us at https://github.com/rexanity/jumpjapanese/issues.

Security

Data stays in the app's sandboxed storage on your device and is protected by your phone's own security (screen lock, device encryption). No method is perfectly secure.

Your rights

Since we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe we do hold something, contact us at https://github.com/rexanity/jumpjapanese/issues and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK, Vietnam, the Philippines, Indonesia), you may have additional rights.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app or the release notes.
